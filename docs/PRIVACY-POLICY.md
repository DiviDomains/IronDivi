# Privacy Policy for IronDivi

**Effective Date:** November 5, 2026

IronDivi is an open-source, decentralized cryptocurrency full node and wallet application. We respect your privacy and are committed to complete transparency.

---

## 1. Zero Telemetry & Zero Personal Data Collection

- **No Analytics / Telemetry**: IronDivi does not contain any analytics SDKs, trackers, or telemetry reporting.
- **No Account Creation**: You do not need to register, provide an email address, or provide any identifying information to install or use IronDivi.
- **No Central Servers**: IronDivi connects directly to the decentralized, peer-to-peer Divi blockchain network. We do not operate custodial relay servers that log your transactions or IP addresses.

---

## 2. Non-Custodial Architecture & Key Management

- **Local Storage**: All private keys, HD wallet seeds, and configuration settings are stored locally on your device in your user directory.
- **Encryption**: Wallet databases containing private keys are encrypted locally using industry-standard cryptography.
- **No Third-Party Access**: IronDivi developers and publishers never have access to your private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or transaction data.

---

## 3. Network Communication

When operating as a full node, IronDivi communicates with other nodes on the peer-to-peer network:
- Peer-to-peer block and transaction exchange occurs over standard consensus ports (e.g. port 51472).
- Your IP address is visible to peer nodes on the public blockchain network, as is intrinsic to all decentralized blockchain protocols. If desired, users may route connections through Tor or SOCKS5 proxies configured in `irondivi.conf`.

---

## 4. Updates & Microsoft Store

When distributed through the Microsoft Store:
- App updates are managed securely by the Microsoft Store service.
- Microsoft's privacy policy governs the download and update metrics processed by Microsoft Partner Center.
