Trade DIVI for other coins, with no exchange in the middle

IronDivi Swaps lets you swap DIVI for Bitcoin (BTC), Litecoin (LTC), Zcash (ZEC), Monero (XMR) or USDC straight from your own wallet. No exchange ever holds your coins. Each trade is an atomic swap: either both sides get what they agreed to, or both sides get their own coins back.

Right now this runs on test networks only. It is a practice run so the software can be proven before any real money is involved.
