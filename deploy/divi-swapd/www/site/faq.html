FAQ

Is this real money?

No. Everything here runs on test networks (Bitcoin signet, Litecoin testnet, Zcash testnet, Monero stagenet, DIVI testnet). The coins have no value. A mainnet version would be announced separately and does not exist yet.

How much can I swap?

Each offer has a smallest and a largest swap, shown here live. Amounts are in whole coins of the other coin.

The maker also limits how fast one address can ask: 20 quotes or new swaps per minute and 300 status reads per minute (bin/divi-swapd/src/routes.rs, lines 51–62). A quote is only valid for 60 seconds.

What does it cost?

The price is the one in the offer; read it as "DIVI per 1 coin of the other chain". On top of that, each lock and each claim pays that chain's normal network fee. The Monero swap also pays a 0.001 DIVI network fee on the DIVI side (crates/divi-swap-xmr/src/lib.rs, line 181).

What can go wrong?

  • A swap can stall if either side goes offline. The refund rules on how it works and safety cover this: you get your own coins back after the deadline.
  • You must be online to claim in time. Do not start a swap you cannot supervise for a few hours.
  • This is new software on test networks. Bugs are expected, which is why it is not on real money.

Testnet or mainnet?

Testnet only. The taker app refuses mainnet unless you pass an extra flag, and this site does not recommend it.
