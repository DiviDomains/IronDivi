How it works and safety

All numbers on this page are the testnet settings used by the code today. A real-money version would use longer waits, and this page would be updated first.

The swap, in plain words (Bitcoin, Litecoin, Zcash, USDC)

This is a hashlock swap. You and the maker each put coins into a lock on your own chain. Both locks open with the same secret word. Only you know the secret to start with.

  1. You lock your coins. They can only be opened with the secret, or taken back by you after a deadline.
  2. The maker sees your lock confirmed and locks its coins the same way, with a shorter deadline.
  3. You use the secret to collect the maker's coins. Doing so shows the secret on that chain.
  4. The maker reads the secret and collects yours.

Your deadline is longer than the maker's on purpose: you always have time to refund after the maker has had its chance to.

The Monero swap, in plain words

Monero cannot hold this kind of lock, so the Monero swap uses adaptor signatures instead. The DIVI side sits in a lock that needs both of your signatures; the Monero side is sent to an address that only you and the maker together can spend. Handing over a signature to collect the DIVI reveals exactly what the maker needs to collect the Monero. If someone stops, a cancel and refund path exists, and a punish path exists for a side that stalls after cancel. You never have to trust the maker to be honest or online for your coins to be safe.

If someone disappears

  • The maker disappears before locking: nothing of yours is spent beyond your own lock. After your deadline, run refund and your coins come back.
  • The maker disappears after locking: you can still collect its coins with claim while its lock is open. If it is too late to claim safely, refund your own side instead.
  • You disappear: the maker takes its coins back after its (shorter) deadline. Nobody can take your coins without the secret, and you keep your refund right until you claim.
  • The website disappears: nothing changes. The swap runs between the taker app and the maker, not through this site.

What this site never does

  • It never asks for keys, seed words, passwords or a sign-in.
  • It has no wallet and cannot move coins.
  • It has no forms, cookies or analytics, and loads nothing from other sites.
  • It only reads two public lists from the maker: its health and its offers.

This site is read-only: no sign-in, no wallet, no keys, no forms, no cookies, no analytics. It only reads healthz and offers from the swap maker. Testnet only.
