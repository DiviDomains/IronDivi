getinvalid 

Get a summary of invalidated outpoints.

Arguments:
1. all   (string, optional) return a full list of outpoints even if they are spent

Examples:
> divi-cli getinvalid "all"
> curl --user myusername --data-binary '{"jsonrpc": "1.0", "id":"curltest", "method": "getinvalid", "params": ["all"] }' -H 'content-type: text/plain;' http://127.0.0.1:51473/
