getaccount "diviaddress"

Returns the account associated with the given address.

Arguments:
1. "diviaddress"  (string, required) The divi address for account lookup.

Result:
"accountname"        (string) the account address

Examples:
> divi-cli getaccount "diviaddress"
> curl --user myusername --data-binary '{"jsonrpc": "1.0", "id":"curltest", "method": "getaccount", "params": ["diviaddress"] }' -H 'content-type: text/plain;' http://127.0.0.1:51473/
