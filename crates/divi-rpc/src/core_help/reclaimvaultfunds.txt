reclaimvaultfunds destination amount (feeMode|metadata) ( "comment" "comment-to" )

Withdraw an amount from your vaults into a separate address. The amount is a real and is rounded to the nearest 0.00000001

Arguments:
1. "diviaddress"  (string, required) The divi address of your choosing to send to.
2. "amount"      (numeric, required) The amount in DIVI to move. eg 0.1

Result:
"transactionid"  (string) The transaction id.
